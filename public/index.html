Recommended for print:
  • Vector art (PDF or SVG) prints sharpest — ideal for logos and text, with fonts outlined/embedded.
  • Raster art (PNG, JPG, TIFF) needs at least 300 DPI at final print size — e.g. an 80×80mm sticker needs a ~945×945px image.
  • Use PNG with a transparent background for custom-cut or irregular shapes.
  • CMYK colour mode gives the truest print match; sRGB is fine if that's not possible.
  • Keep a small bleed (2mm+) beyond your cut line, and keep key text 2mm inside it.
  • Max file size 10MB.
